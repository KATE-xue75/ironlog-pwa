The Problem: Can't Make Noise, Can't Have Equipment

You live in an apartment. You have downstairs neighbors. Your landlord said "no gym equipment." Your floor space is 2 square meters.

Most workout plans assume you have a garage gym with barbells, a bench, and the freedom to drop weights. They don't work for renters, apartment dwellers, travelers, or anyone sharing walls.

🏢 37% of Americans rent their homes. Hundreds of millions more live in apartments globally. Yet fitness content is overwhelmingly made for garage-gym owners.

Renter-Friendly Training Principles

  1. Land softly — All bodyweight movements use controlled descents. No plyometric jumps that shake the floor.
  2. Zero equipment — Floor space is all you need. A yoga mat is nice but optional.
  3. Auto-swap noisy movements — IronLog automatically suggests quiet alternatives: push-ups instead of barbell bench press, glute bridges instead of deadlifts.
  4. Train anytime — Midnight workout? No problem. No clanking weights, no treadmill noise.
