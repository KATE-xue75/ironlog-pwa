Honest Answers

Q: Why is this actually free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. This app runs entirely in your browser's LocalStorage — zero database syncing. Hosting it on Cloudflare Pages costs me exactly $0. Since I don't pay to store your data, I don't need to charge you a subscription.

Q: Are there ads or tracker scripts?

Zero. No Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no adware. We respect your attention and your battery life.

Q: How do I back up my data?

Hit Export in the app. You'll get a clean CSV/JSON file instantly. Import it back on any device. No accounts needed.
