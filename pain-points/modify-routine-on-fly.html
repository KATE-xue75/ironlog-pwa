Your Training Plan Should Adapt to Reality

Most workout apps treat your routine as a rigid, unchangeable script. Your plan says "dips" — but the dip bars at the park are occupied. Your shoulder feels tight. You're training at home today instead of the gym.

In the real world, you need to adapt. But most apps force you to either skip the exercise entirely (messing up your tracking) or exit the workout, edit the template, and restart.

🎯 The problem: Life happens mid-workout. Equipment is taken. Joints feel off. You're in a different location. Your app should adapt as fast as you do.

IronLog: One-Tap Exercise Swaps, Mid-Workout

IronLog treats your routine as a living document. During any workout:

  1. Tap the exercise — the current movement in your routine
  2. Choose "Swap" — IronLog shows relevant alternatives (same muscle group, similar difficulty, compatible equipment)
  3. Pick the replacement — the swap is recorded for this session only (your template stays unchanged for next time)
  4. Keep training — sets, reps, and rest timers continue seamlessly

Common Real-World Swaps

SituationOriginal ExerciseOne-Tap Swap
Dip bars occupiedParallel Bar Dips→ Bench Dips or Pike Push-ups
Pull-up bar not availablePull-ups→ Doorframe Rows or Inverted Rows under table
Shoulder feels tightHandstand Push-ups→ Pike Push-ups (reduced ROM)
Training at home (no equipment)Barbell Squat→ Bulgarian Split Squat (bodyweight)
Wrist pain acting upPush-ups (flat palm)→ Fist Push-ups or Parallettes Push-ups

Also: Reorder Your Routine Anytime

Want to do core work first because you're fresh? Move the core triplet before strength pairs. Prefer to warm up differently? Reorder the warm-up sequence. IronLog's template editor gives you full drag-and-drop control over exercise order.

Changes persist across sessions until you reset to the original template — so your custom version becomes your routine.
