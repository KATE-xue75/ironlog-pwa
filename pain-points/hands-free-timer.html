You Should Not Need to Stare at a Screen During Planks

Think about it: you're in a plank position. Your arms are shaking. Your core is on fire. And now you have to crane your neck sideways to check how many seconds are left on your phone timer?

Or you're doing handstand practice against a wall. You're inverted. Your phone is on the floor. You can't reach it, can't see it, can't hear the tiny beep over your breathing.

This is absurd. And it's the standard experience across almost every fitness app.

🎯 The problem: Most workout timers require visual confirmation. During bodyweight exercises — especially static holds like planks, L-sits, and handstands — you physically cannot look at your phone.

IronLog: Auto Rest Timer That Works Without Looking

IronLog handles timing automatically. Here's how it works:

  1. Log your set — tap once to mark it complete
  2. Timer starts automatically — no need to open a separate timer app
