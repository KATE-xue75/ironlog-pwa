They're Charging You for a Free Routine. That's Not Right.

The Recommended Routine was created by the Reddit community — for free, for everyone. It's a public good. Yet dozens of apps have wrapped it in a paywall, charging $5-15/month for what is essentially a UI on top of freely available information.

Worse, many of these apps add the exact things the community hates: social feeds, push notifications, "challenges," leaderboards, premium-only features that lock basic functionality.

💡 The RR community's stance is clear: don't monetize the routine. Make it accessible. Keep it clean. IronLog follows this principle to the letter.

How IronLog Stays Free (Without Selling Your Data)

Simple: it costs $0/month to run. IronLog has no backend server. All your data lives in your browser's localStorage — we don't have a database to maintain, a server to pay for, or user accounts to manage.

Hosting is on Cloudflare Pages (free tier). Exercise GIFs come from ExerciseDB's free tier. The entire infrastructure bill is $0.00/month.

Premium training templates (Bodyweight Mastery, Desk Warrior, Travel Fit) are available as optional one-time purchases — not subscriptions. They're extras, not the core experience. The RR stays free forever.
