The RR Wiki Is Brilliant. It's Also Impossible to Use During a Workout.

The r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine is the gold standard of bodyweight training. It's been refined over a decade by the community. It works. Millions of people have built strength with it.

But the wiki page? It's a nightmare to use mid-workout. You need to:

  1. Open the wiki in one tab
  2. Open YouTube in another tab for exercise demos
  3. Open a notes app to track your sets and reps
  4. Open a timer app for rest periods
  5. Juggle all four while trying to actually exercise

Every session starts with "wait, what's the next pair? Was it pull-ups + squats or dips + hinges?"

What the RR Template Includes

  1. Warm-up — wrist circles, shoulder rolls, scapular shrugs, cat-cow, etc.
  2. Skill work — handstand progression, support hold (paired)
  3. Strength — First Pair — pull-up progression + squat progression (3 sets each, alternating)
  4. Strength — Second Pair — dip progression + hinge progression (3 sets each, alternating)
  5. Strength — Third Pair — row progression + push-up progression (3 sets each, alternating)
  6. Core Triplet — anti-extension + anti-rotation + extension (3 rounds)

Every exercise comes with a progression ladder (easiest → hardest) so you always know what to work toward next.
