# IronLog — Free, No-Login, No-Ads Workout Tracker

**IronLog** is a 100% local-first Progressive Web App (PWA) workout tracker. No account. No ads. No subscription. Your data never leaves your browser.

## Why IronLog

- **No login required** — just open and start logging
- **No ads** — zero ad SDKs, zero tracking scripts
- **100% local-first** — all data stored in your browser's localStorage
- **One-click CSV export** — your data, your rules. Take it anywhere
- **PWA** — install to home screen, works completely offline
- **Reddit r/bodyweightfitness Recommended Routine (RR) built-in**
- **57+ exercises** with animated GIFs and step-by-step instructions
- **Open source (MIT)** — audit the code, fork it, host your own

## Tech Stack

- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (no framework)
- PWA with Service Worker for offline support
- Hosted on **Cloudflare Pages** (zero-cost, global CDN)
- Exercise GIFs from ExerciseDB (AscendAPI Free Tier)
- Premium templates sold via Gumroad

## Privacy

- **Your data stays on your device** — IronLog has no backend, no database, no server
- **No tracking** — no analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts
- **CSV export** — download your workout history anytime
- **MIT license** — free to use, modify, and self-host

## Why I Built This

I was tired of fitness apps that cost $14.99/month, require an account, push ads, and lock your data behind a paywall. IronLog is the opposite: free forever, no strings attached. Premium templates are optional and one-time — support if you find it useful, ignore if you don't.

Built with Cloudflare Pages (zero hosting cost) so there's literally no reason to charge you a subscription.
